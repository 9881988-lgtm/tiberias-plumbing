Tnufa and accelerator application support

Bring us your idea. We prepare and submit applications to Tnufa and to other accelerators that fit your project. We select a programme, assemble the documents and submit the package once you approve it.

What we do

01

Program fit

We review the project stage, published requirements, deadlines and supporting documents. We look at the Israel Innovation Authority’s Tnufa program and relevant accelerators and incubators.

02

Project development

We define the problem and technical novelty, scope a prototype or proof of concept, and set milestones and validation criteria.

03

Application preparation

We draft the technical description, R&D plan and budget using your information. We prepare a pitch deck, summary and attachments as required.

04

Submission and follow-up

We submit the approved application on your behalf where the programme permits it. If only the founder can submit, we prepare the complete file and guide you through the final step.

We are an independent service provider. Admission and grant decisions are made by the program organizer. No approval or funding is guaranteed.
