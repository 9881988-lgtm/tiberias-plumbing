BEFORE WE START

A few good questions.

Can we start with a small project?

Yes. A focused landing page, a product prototype or a single automation pilot can be a sensible first engagement. We agree the scope before work begins.

Do you work outside Israel?

Yes. Digital work can be delivered remotely. Our site is available in Hebrew, English, Russian and French; project language, time zone and collaboration arrangements are agreed at the start.

Can you improve an existing website or app?

Yes. We first review the existing code, access, content and constraints, then propose a realistic improvement plan instead of assuming everything needs rebuilding.

What do I receive at the end?

The deliverables, handover, access, ownership terms and any maintenance are defined in the written proposal. App Store approval, advertising results and search rankings cannot be guaranteed.
