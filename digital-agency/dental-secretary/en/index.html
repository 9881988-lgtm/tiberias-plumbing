Controlled launch and privacy

Customer telephony is at pilot stage: connect a number and test language, routing and human handoff. CRM and calendar access are not automatic. Before processing patient information, agree permissions, data minimisation, notices and retention. The assistant does not diagnose, recommend treatment or replace emergency care. Medical questions go to the clinic.

The aim is fewer interruptions and more organised enquiry handling. We do not promise zero missed calls, a guaranteed number of patients, higher ratings or search positions.

Before you subscribe

Is this a human receptionist?

No. It is a voice AI receptionist configured for the clinic. Human escalation and availability of your responsible team member are agreed separately.

What happens after 100 minutes?

The monthly fee includes 100 incoming call minutes. Excess actual call duration costs ILS 18/hour, equivalent to ILS 0.30/minute.

Is there a setup fee?

No separate setup fee for the agreed website, basic CRM configuration and map profiles. External costs and out-of-scope work require separate agreement.

How much weekly content is included?

One shared topic adapted for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and X, plus one short YouTube video. Not five independent content campaigns.

Can you book in our system?

We check the supported CRM/calendar connection and clinic permissions. Compatibility with every dental or medical system is not promised before testing.

Are patients and high ratings guaranteed?

No. We deliver agreed work, not guaranteed sales, patient numbers, ratings, rankings or AI recommendations. We do not buy or fabricate reviews.
