Sales desk example

From an email to a quote

  1. An enquiry arrives

    A customer emails a list of items. The system extracts each line and flags missing details.

  2. A quote is drafted

    Items are matched to your approved price list. If no price is found, the line is left for review.

  3. The sales rep decides

    The rep checks prices and terms. Only after approval does the system send the quote and record the next action in the CRM.

Is the workflow worth automating?

Pick one frequent task. Record volume, handling time and errors before changing anything. Test on de-identified examples, then compare the same measures, including review time and running costs.

Illustration, not a forecast: 120 enquiries per month × 5 net minutes saved per enquiry = 10 team hours per month. If the benefit does not cover the cost, do not scale the pilot.

What we mean by “business superintelligence”

This is not a general-purpose superintelligence. We choose models for specific tasks and use Codex to build and test the software connecting them to your tools. Where a simple rule is enough, we use one. Before launch we agree on access, retention and activity logs. People approve payments, exceptional discounts and other consequential decisions, and can switch back to manual processing.
