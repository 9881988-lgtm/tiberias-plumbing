Privacy Policy

Edelstein CRM Israel — iPhone, iPad and Mac

Summary

Edelstein CRM Israel does not send your customers, deals, tasks, appointments or business settings to Edelstein Digital. The app contains no third-party advertising SDK, no cross-app tracking and no developer analytics SDK.

Where your data is stored

Records are stored locally on your device for offline use. When iCloud is available, Apple CloudKit synchronizes the database through the private iCloud account signed in on your devices. Edelstein Digital cannot browse your private CloudKit database. Apple processes iCloud data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Data you enter

You may voluntarily enter names, company names, phone numbers, email addresses, notes, deal values, tasks and appointments. You control this business data and are responsible for having a lawful basis to store and contact each person. Do not enter passwords, payment-card data, medical data or government identity documents.

Our in-app promotion

The dashboard may show a clearly labeled Edelstein Digital house advertisement. It can be dismissed. Only the interface language selected on the device is used to choose its text. Customer records, business records, location, contacts and device advertising identifiers are not used. The app does not use ATT/IDFA.

Import, links and permissions

The optional JSON importer reads only the file you select. Phone, email, WhatsApp, Waze and website buttons open the relevant external app or website after you tap them. Those services apply their own privacy policies. The CRM does not request access to your system address book, microphone, camera, photos or location.

Retention and deletion

Records remain until you delete or archive them. To remove synced data, delete records in the app on a device connected to iCloud. You can also remove the app's iCloud data from your Apple account settings and uninstall the app. A dismissed house-ad preference is stored in UserDefaults on the device.

Support and changes

Questions or deletion assistance: 9881988@gmail.com. We may update this policy when the app adds a feature or changes its data practices. Material changes will be reflected here and in App Store privacy disclosures.

תקציר בעברית

האפליקציה שומרת את נתוני ה-CRM במכשיר וב-iCloud הפרטי של המשתמש. הנתונים אינם נשלחים ל-Edelstein Digital, אין SDK פרסום של צד שלישי ואין מעקב. הפרסומת הפנימית משתמשת רק בשפת הממשק שנבחרה. ניתן למחוק רשומות באפליקציה או להסיר את נתוני האפליקציה מ-iCloud.

Кратко по-русски

CRM хранит данные локально и в личном iCloud пользователя. Данные клиентов не передаются Edelstein Digital; сторонней рекламной сети, аналитики и отслеживания нет. Собственный баннер использует только выбранный язык интерфейса. Записи можно удалить в приложении или удалить данные приложения из iCloud.
