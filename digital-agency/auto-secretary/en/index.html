Controlled launch and service boundaries

Customer telephony is at pilot stage. Test language, number, routing and human handoff before launch. Connect and test CRM or calendar access separately. Agree permissions, data minimisation and retention. The assistant does not diagnose faults or confirm that a vehicle is safe to drive. Your workshop confirms the diagnosis, final price, parts availability and completion time. Dangerous situations go to your team or an appropriate emergency service.

The aim is fewer interruptions and more organised enquiry handling. We do not promise zero missed calls, a guaranteed number of jobs, higher ratings or search positions.

Before you subscribe

Is this a human receptionist?

No. It is a voice AI receptionist configured for your workshop. Agree escalation and availability of your responsible team member separately.

What happens after 100 minutes?

The monthly fee includes 100 incoming call minutes. Excess actual call duration costs ILS 18/hour, equivalent to ILS 0.30/minute.

Is there a setup fee?

No separate setup fee for the agreed website, basic CRM configuration and map profiles. External costs and out-of-scope work require separate agreement.

How much weekly content is included?

One shared topic adapted for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and X, plus one short YouTube video. Not five independent content campaigns.

Can you book in our system?

We check the supported CRM/calendar connection and workshop permissions. Compatibility with every system or live repair status is not promised before testing.

Can AI quote the final repair price?

It shares approved prices and conditions only. Your team confirms the final quote, diagnosis, safety, parts and completion time. Sales, ratings and AI recommendations are not guaranteed.
