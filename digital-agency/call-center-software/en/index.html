How to start a subscription

  1. Demo

    Map one real business workflow without submitting sensitive customer information in a public form.

  2. Scope and plan

    Agree channels, integrations, AI permissions, acceptance criteria and total costs.

  3. Configure and pilot

    Use an approved catalog and test language, human escalation and connected channels.

  4. Accept and launch

    Agree launch after verification. Subscription, support and cancellation terms are documented in the agreement.

Connections are part of implementation

Telephony, WhatsApp, Google Calendar, Booking.com and Airbnb are not connected automatically. Availability depends on supported APIs, owner permissions and successful testing. Email reports have been verified in the current owner configuration; your business requires separate setup. AI can make mistakes; verify important information and decisions. We do not promise autonomous superintelligence, guaranteed sales or replacement of every employee.

Before you connect

Is the call center separate from the CRM?

It can be implemented as a focused workflow, but it is a Galany CRM module using the same data and access rules.

Can I forward all calls immediately?

No. Production customer telephony is still in pilot. Agree the provider, number and routing, then pass an acceptance test first.

How are calls billed?

Light and Booking: ILS 18 per actual call-hour, equivalent to ILS 0.30/minute. Business: the first line is billed 24/7 for a full month including idle time, ILS 12,960 for 30 days. Prices exclude VAT.

Can it handle Hebrew and Russian?

Choose and test the language with your business vocabulary during the pilot. Equal quality across every language, accent or noisy call is not promised without testing.
