Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Focentra, Docuvail, Autorythm, Packlume, Brewmetry, Beaconora, Formcrest, Daylune, Doughclock, and Privashade for iPhone.

Data collection

We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or use personal data for advertising or tracking. The apps do not require an account and do not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

On-device information

Information you enter or create—including focus history, PDFs, vehicle records, packing lists, coffee recipes, BLE scan results, body measurements, journal entries, bake plans, and redacted photos—is processed locally on your device. We cannot access it.

Device permissions

  • Camera: Docuvail uses the camera when you start a document scan.
  • Photos: Privashade uses selected images for on-device redaction and saves a copy only when you choose to export.
  • Bluetooth: Beaconora reads nearby BLE advertisements during a scan you start.

The operating system controls these permissions. You can change them at any time in iPhone Settings.

Purchases

Lifetime purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive entitlement status, not your payment card details. Apple's own terms and privacy policy apply to App Store transactions.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them in the app or remove the app. Because we do not hold a server copy, we cannot recover or delete the data remotely.

Children

The apps do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app functionality changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Questions can be sent to 9881988@gmail.com.
