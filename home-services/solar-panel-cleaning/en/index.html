Solar panel washing for power plants

We wash photovoltaic modules at ground-mounted solar farms and commercial rooftops in northern Israel. Plant operators, farms and property managers can request a single wash or a series of visits.

How we scope a wash

With the site operator, we review row layout, access lanes, water supply and the module manufacturer’s cleaning instructions. We agree on the cleaning method and work area before the visit.

This service covers the surface of electricity-generating panels. It excludes electrical repairs and solar water-heater collectors. No specific energy yield increase is guaranteed.

Budgeting for the work

Israeli pricing guides indicate about ₪7–9 before VAT per installed kilowatt for one wash of a commercial system. At 100 kW, that gives an illustrative ₪700–900 before VAT for one visit.

This is a market example, not a fixed tariff. For a large plant or seasonal contract, we quote for the layout, access, water supply, soiling and visit frequency.

Market price sources: Midrag · SunLink

What to send for a quote

Send the location, installed capacity in kW or module count, whether the array is on a roof or the ground, and whether water is available. Include an overview photo and your preferred date.
