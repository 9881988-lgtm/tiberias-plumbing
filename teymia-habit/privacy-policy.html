Teymia Habit Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Overview

Teymia Habit is designed as a private, on-device habit tracker. We do not require an account, run advertising, or use cross-app tracking.

Data collection

We do not collect personal data, habit entries, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, or health data.

Local storage

Your habits, completion dates, language selection, and free-access start date are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app may remove this local data.

Purchases

The optional lifetime purchase is processed by Apple using StoreKit. We receive purchase entitlement status from Apple so the app can unlock paid features. Apple handles payment information under its own privacy terms.

Third parties

Teymia Habit does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not sell or share user data.

Children

Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
