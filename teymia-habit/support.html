Teymia Habit Support

Teymia Habit is a private daily habit and streak tracker for iPhone.

Getting started

  • Choose your interface language on first launch.
  • Tap the plus button to create a habit and choose its schedule.
  • Mark completed days from the main calendar.
  • Open a habit to review progress and streak analytics.

Purchase

The app includes 7 days of free access. After that period, a one-time $4.99 purchase unlocks lifetime access. There is no subscription. Purchases and restores are processed by Apple through the App Store.

Restore a purchase

On the unlock screen, tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
