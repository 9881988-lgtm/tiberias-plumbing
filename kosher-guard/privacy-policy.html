Kosher Guard Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 28, 2026

Kosher Guard is a free iPhone app that helps users prepare a kosher phone policy and understand supervised MDM requirements.

Data Collection

Kosher Guard does not collect personal data, does not track users, and does not sell or share user data with third parties.

Local Data

Language choice, policy notes, and request drafts are stored locally on the user's device. Kosher Guard does not upload this information to our servers.

Accounts and Payments

Kosher Guard does not require an account. The app is free and does not include subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Network and Servers

Kosher Guard is designed as a local planning tool. It does not send app usage, policy settings, or request notes to a developer-operated server.
