Kosher Guard Support

Free kosher phone policy helper for iPhone.

What Kosher Guard Does

  • Helps families, schools, and communities prepare a kosher phone policy.
  • Shows common app risk categories before creating an allowlist.
  • Helps prepare app and website request notes.
  • Explains when supervised MDM is needed for stronger device-wide enforcement.

Important Limit

Kosher Guard does not claim to fully control an iPhone by itself. Full device-wide enforcement requires supervised MDM or Apple-approved Screen Time distribution.

Cost

Kosher Guard is free. There are no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.
