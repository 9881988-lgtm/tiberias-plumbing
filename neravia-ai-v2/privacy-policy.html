Privacy Policy

Effective August 4, 2026

NoCloud AI is designed to work locally by default and does not require an account.

Data collection

NoCloud AI does not receive or store the contents of your documents, task text, generated results, provider API keys, contacts, account details, or precise location. The free version uses Google AdMob to show non-personalized advertising. Google's advertising SDK may process an IP-derived approximate location, device or app identifiers, advertising data, product interaction, diagnostics, and performance data to deliver ads, prevent fraud, and measure service reliability. NoCloud AI disables ad personalization and the Google publisher first-party identifier.

Where required, the app presents Google's privacy choices before requesting an ad. You can review those choices from Settings. The paid version does not show ads.

Documents and generated content

Files you import, local indexes, preferences, and generated results are processed and stored inside the app container on your device. Deleting the app removes this local app data according to iOS behavior.

Optional model downloads

If you explicitly choose a Gemma model, the app connects to the model host over HTTPS and stores the downloaded model locally. Available models range from the compact Gemma 3 270M to larger Gemma 4 editions on compatible devices. The app does not upload your prompts or documents to the model host.

Optional external AI providers

Settings includes an optional bring-your-own-key mode for Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, and Hugging Face. It is disabled by default. Saving a key does not enable it. Before enabling it, the app identifies the selected provider, explains the data transfer, links to that provider's privacy policy, and asks for permission.

If you tap “Allow and enable,” the text you enter in the task field and your API key are sent directly from your device to the selected provider over HTTPS for authentication and AI processing. The provider may also receive your IP address and ordinary network metadata. Imported documents and saved results are not sent. NoCloud AI does not operate a remote inference service and does not receive or store this data. The selected provider's privacy policy and terms apply.

Apple services and purchases

The optional built-in Apple model and subscription purchases use Apple system frameworks. Purchases are processed by the App Store and governed by Apple’s privacy terms.

Children

The app is not designed to collect information from anyone, including children.
