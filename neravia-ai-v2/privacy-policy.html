Privacy Policy

Effective July 16, 2026

NoCloud AI is designed to work locally and does not require an account.

Data collection

We do not collect personal data, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, diagnostics, or the contents of your documents. We do not sell or share personal data and do not track you across apps or websites.

Documents and generated content

Files you import, local indexes, preferences, and generated results are processed and stored inside the app container on your device. Deleting the app removes this local app data according to iOS behavior.

Optional model downloads

If you explicitly choose a Gemma model, the app connects to the model host over HTTPS and stores the downloaded model locally. Available models range from the compact Gemma 3 270M to larger Gemma 4 editions on compatible devices. The app does not upload your prompts or documents to the model host.

Apple services and purchases

The optional built-in Apple model and subscription purchases use Apple system frameworks. Purchases are processed by the App Store and governed by Apple’s privacy terms.

Children

The app is not designed to collect information from anyone, including children.
