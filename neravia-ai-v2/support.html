Support

NoCloud AI works offline-first and requires no account.

Documents

Supported imports: PDF, TXT, Markdown, RTF, JSON, and CSV. Scanned PDFs without a text layer may not contain readable text.

Local models

Start with Gemma 3 270M, a separate download of about 280 MB. Larger Gemma 3 and Gemma 4 models are offered only when the device has enough memory and storage. The Mac edition also supports larger models for Apple Silicon.

Storage

You can remove imported documents and downloaded models from inside the app. iOS, iPadOS, and macOS may also manage app resources according to system storage rules.
