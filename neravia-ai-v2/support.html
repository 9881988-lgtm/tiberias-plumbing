Support

Neravia AI works offline-first and requires no account.

Documents

Supported imports: PDF, TXT, Markdown, RTF, JSON, and CSV. Scanned PDFs without a text layer may not contain readable text.

Optional Bonsai 27B model

The model is a separate download of approximately 5.16 GB and needs a compatible high-memory iPhone. If your device does not meet the memory threshold, Neravia keeps the model disabled to protect stability and temperature.

Storage

You can remove imported documents and the downloaded model from inside the app. iOS may also reclaim app resources according to system storage rules.
