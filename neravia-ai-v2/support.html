Support

NoCloud AI requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later. A NoCloud AI account is not required.

Documents

Supported imports are PDF, TXT, Markdown, RTF, CSV, and JSON. The current version does not provide OCR for scanned PDFs, folder-wide search, or source citations.

Local models

Selected Gemma models download separately. The smallest model is about 280 MB. After a compatible model is installed, local generation can continue without an internet connection.

Free and Pro

The free version shows a short screen before a request. Optional Pro at $0.99 per month removes that screen.

Accuracy

Local AI can make mistakes. Verify important names, numbers, and conclusions against the source, and do not rely on it for professional legal, financial, or medical review.
