DocuLuma Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

DocuLuma is a private document scanning app for iPhone. It is designed to work locally on your device and avoid unnecessary data collection.

Data Collection

DocuLuma does not collect personal data, does not track users, and does not sell or share user data with third parties.

Documents and Scans

Scanned documents and imported photos are stored locally on your device. DocuLuma does not upload your documents to our servers. PDF export is initiated by you through the iOS share sheet.

Permissions

  • Camera access is used to scan documents into PDF files.
  • Photo library access is used only when you choose images to import.
  • Face ID is optional and is used only to lock and unlock your local document library.

In-App Purchases

DocuLuma offers a one-time lifetime unlock through Apple In-App Purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the developer contact information provided with the App Store listing.
