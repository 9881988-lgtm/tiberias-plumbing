DocuLuma Support

DocuLuma is a simple PDF scanner for iPhone.

Common Tasks

  • Use Scan to capture new paper documents.
  • Use Import to convert selected photos into PDF documents.
  • Open a document to rotate pages, apply filters, delete pages, or export PDF.
  • After 5 free pages, use Lifetime Unlock to enable unlimited scanning and PDF export.

Restore Purchase

If you already purchased Lifetime Unlock, open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchase.

Contact

For help, use the developer contact information provided with the App Store listing.
