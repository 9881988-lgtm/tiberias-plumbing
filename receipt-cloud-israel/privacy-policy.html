Receipt Cloud Israel Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Receipt Cloud Israel is a receipt record app for one Israeli business. The app is designed to keep receipt data under the user's control.

Data Stored by the App

The app stores business profile details, client details, receipt records, yearly totals, and generated PDF files on the user's device. When iCloud is available and enabled for the user's Apple ID, app data may also sync through the user's private Apple iCloud account.

Data Collection

The developer does not collect, sell, or share receipt contents, business records, client records, or PDF files. The app does not send receipt data to a developer-owned server.

iCloud

iCloud sync is provided by Apple and is tied to the user's Apple ID. The user can manage iCloud availability and storage in iOS Settings. iCloud data is subject to Apple's iCloud terms and privacy practices.

Sharing

If the user chooses to export or share a PDF receipt through WhatsApp, Mail, Files, or another service, that action is initiated by the user and is handled by the selected service.

In-App Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple App Store. The developer does not receive or store credit card numbers, Apple ID passwords, or payment credentials.

Professional Disclaimer

Receipt Cloud Israel is a practical record-keeping tool. It is not a substitute for official tax authority requirements, accounting advice, legal advice, or certified electronic-signature services.
