Israel Receipt Ledger Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Israel Receipt Ledger is a local record-keeping app for one Israeli business. No account is required, and receipt data remains under the user's control.

Data Stored by the App

The app stores the business profile, client details entered by the user, receipt records, yearly totals, purchase-access flags, and the integrity ledger locally on the user's device. Generated PDF files are created locally for export or sharing. The app does not automatically synchronize this data to iCloud, Google Drive, or a developer-owned server.

Data Collection

The developer does not collect, sell, or share receipt contents, business records, client records, PDF files, precise location, contacts, identifiers, diagnostics, or usage analytics. The app contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or tracking technology.

Optional Google Drive Backup

Backup and restore are manual, user-initiated actions. When the user taps Save Backup or Restore Backup, the iOS Files picker opens. If the Google Drive app is installed and the user selects Google Drive, Apple and Google transfer the selected backup file on the user's behalf. The app and developer do not receive Google credentials and do not have background access to the user's Google Drive. Files stored in Google Drive are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

If the user chooses to export or share a PDF receipt through WhatsApp, Mail, Files, or another service, that action is initiated by the user and is handled by the selected service.

Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on the device until the user removes the app or erases the device. The developer retains no copy and therefore cannot retrieve or delete it remotely. Exported PDFs and backup files remain in the destination selected by the user until the user deletes them there. A restored backup replaces the app's local record data but never restores App Store purchase entitlements.

In-App Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple App Store. The developer does not receive or store credit card numbers, Apple ID passwords, or payment credentials.

Professional Disclaimer

Israel Receipt Ledger is a practical record-keeping tool. It is not a substitute for official tax authority requirements, accounting advice, legal advice, or certified electronic-signature services.
