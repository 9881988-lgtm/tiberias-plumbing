Offline Budget Support

Privacy Policy

Effective August 4, 2026

Offline Budget: Private Money is designed for private, on-device money tracking. No account is required.

Data we collect

The developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share your budget entries, transactions, categories, notes, preferences, contacts, precise location, or identifiers.

Local storage

Your financial records and settings are stored locally inside the app container on your device. Offline Budget does not upload them to the developer, Apple iCloud, Google Cloud, or another cloud service. Deleting the app removes its local data according to iOS behavior.

Exports

If you choose to export or share information, iOS presents the system share sheet and you select the destination. Offline Budget does not receive a copy.

Purchases

The optional one-time lifetime Pro purchase and purchase restoration are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. There is no subscription or automatic renewal. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process purchase and account information under Apple's privacy policy.

External pages

Opening this policy, the support page, or Apple's Terms of Use uses your browser. The website provider may receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address and browser request metadata.

Children

Offline Budget does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.
