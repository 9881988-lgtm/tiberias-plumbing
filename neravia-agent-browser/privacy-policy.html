PRIVACY POLICY

Neravia Agent Browser

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Neravia Agent Browser does not include advertising, analytics or tracking, and the developer does not receive your browsing content, prompts or API key.

Information stored on your Mac

Your TokenRouter API key is stored in macOS Keychain. Agent session summaries and app preferences are stored locally in the app's sandboxed container. They are not uploaded to the developer.

TokenRouter processing

When you run an AI agent, your goal, conversation messages and bounded excerpts from visible webpages are sent directly from the app to TokenRouter so the selected Kimi K3 model can plan and answer. This processing is initiated by you and is governed by TokenRouter's privacy policy and terms. Do not include information you do not want processed by that provider.

Web browsing

Websites opened in the built-in WebKit browser receive ordinary network information such as your IP address and may use cookies or other storage according to their own policies. You control which pages are opened. The app does not add advertising identifiers.

Optional Lightpanda connection

If you enable a Lightpanda MCP endpoint, browser tool requests and results are exchanged with the server address you configure. The developer does not operate or receive data from that endpoint. Remote Lightpanda connections must use HTTPS; local HTTP is limited to this Mac.

Data sharing and sales

The developer does not sell personal information and does not share data for advertising. Data is sent only to services you explicitly configure or websites you choose to visit as required for app functionality.

Deletion

You can remove the API key from Settings and clear local session history at any time. Deleting the app removes its sandboxed local data subject to normal macOS and backup behavior.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information listed on the app's support page.
