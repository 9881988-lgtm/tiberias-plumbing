PRIVACY POLICY

Neravia Agent Browser

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Neravia Agent Browser does not include accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking. The developer does not receive your browsing content, prompts or session history.

On-device AI processing

Agent planning uses Apple's Foundation Models framework and runs on your compatible Mac. Goals and bounded excerpts from visible webpages are processed by the on-device model. The app does not send them to the developer or an external AI provider.

Information stored on your Mac

Agent session summaries and app preferences are stored locally in the app's sandboxed container. They are not uploaded to the developer. The app does not require an account, API key or AI subscription.

Web browsing

Websites opened in the built-in WebKit browser receive ordinary network information such as your IP address and may use cookies or other storage according to their own policies. You control which pages are opened. The app does not add advertising identifiers.

Data sharing and sales

The developer does not collect, sell or share personal information for advertising. Network requests go only to websites you choose to visit as required for browser functionality.

Deletion

You can clear local session history from the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its sandboxed local data subject to normal macOS and backup behavior.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information listed on the app's support page.
