IvriCards Support

IvriCards helps you study Hebrew with offline flashcards, transliteration, pronunciation, search, and progress tracking.

Common Help

  • Choose your interface language on first launch.
  • Use search to find any Hebrew word in the 500-card deck.
  • Tap Listen to hear pronunciation and Auto Play for hands-free practice.
  • Use Restore Purchase if you already bought the lifetime unlock with the same Apple ID.

Purchases

IvriCards includes two days of access. After that, a one-time Apple In-App Purchase unlocks all cards and study tools forever.

Contact

For support questions, use the developer contact information provided with the App Store listing.
