IvriCards Support

IvriCards 2.0 combines 365 daily foundation practice sessions, 300 core Hebrew cards, reading support, spaced repetition and saved progress in one app.

Common Help

  • Choose a vocabulary language on first launch. Meanings are available in 20 languages; detailed grammar explanations currently use English or Russian.
  • Open Course for daily practice, Cards for spaced repetition, Reading for the alphabet and introductory vowel marks, or Dictionary to search. The previous 500-word dictionary remains available separately.
  • Finish a session and tap Complete and save to record completion. Card practice saves your next review date after Again or Known.
  • Tap Listen for Hebrew pronunciation. Speech uses the iOS Hebrew voice. Install that voice while online before relying on offline audio; audio is not a bundle of recorded human speech.
  • Progress is stored on this device, without a developer-operated sync service. Deleting the app can delete progress. Restoring a purchase restores access, not deleted learning history.

Purchases

Core card practice and the first eight sessions are free. Two days of included full access let you explore the course. An optional one-time lifetime purchase opens all 365 sessions permanently, without a subscription. Existing IvriCards lifetime purchases remain valid.

To buy or restore, open Settings (gear tab) → Lifetime access / restore. Unlock forever and Restore purchases are on the same screen, including during the free period. Purchase and restore require internet and the Apple account used for the original purchase. If the price is unavailable, check your connection and tap Reload; the app never substitutes an invented price.

Previous versions

These instructions describe version 2.0. The previous 1.x release used the 500-card deck and a different study screen. Purchases from separate apps cannot be transferred automatically through Apple; contact support before buying again.
