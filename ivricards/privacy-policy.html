IvriCards Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

IvriCards is a Hebrew flashcard learning app for iPhone. It is designed to work locally on your device and avoid unnecessary data collection.

Data Collection

IvriCards does not collect personal data, does not track users, and does not sell or share user data with third parties.

Study Progress

Your selected language, known-card progress, and study preferences are stored locally on your device. IvriCards does not upload this information to our servers.

Audio and Learning Content

Hebrew words, translations, transliteration, and audio playback features are provided inside the app. Audio playback and search work locally after installation.

In-App Purchases

IvriCards offers a one-time lifetime unlock through Apple In-App Purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the developer contact information provided with the App Store listing.
