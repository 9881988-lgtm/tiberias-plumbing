IvriCards Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

IvriCards is a Hebrew learning app for iPhone and iPad. Learning content and progress are designed to work locally on your device.

Data Collection

IvriCards does not collect personal data, does not track users, and does not sell or share user data with third parties.

Study Progress

Your selected language, lesson completion, card review schedule and study preferences are stored locally on your device. IvriCards does not upload this information to our servers and does not operate a cloud sync service. Your device backup settings are controlled by you and Apple. Deleting the app may delete local progress; restoring a purchase does not restore deleted progress.

Audio and Learning Content

Words, translations, reading material and search are included in the app. Pronunciation uses Apple's on-device Hebrew speech voice; that voice must be installed before offline use. IvriCards does not use an external AI service, request microphone access, or send your typed practice answers to a server.

In-App Purchases

IvriCards offers a one-time lifetime unlock through Apple In-App Purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.

The app checks purchase entitlements with Apple's StoreKit. Buying and restoring require an internet connection. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs in IvriCards.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email 9881988@gmail.com. If you contact us, the information you choose to include is used to answer your request; it is not collected automatically by the app. Do not include passwords or payment-card details.
