Neravia AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Summary

Neravia AI is designed as a private offline-first assistant. The compact assistant runs on device, and prompts are not sent to our servers by this app.

Data Collection

Neravia AI does not collect personal data for tracking or advertising. The app privacy manifest declares no collected data types and no tracking.

Local Content

Prompts, drafts, summaries, translations, and app settings are processed on the device. If local history or saved content is enabled, it stays on the iPhone unless the user exports or shares it manually.

Purchases

Trials, purchases, and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Neravia AI does not receive or store payment card information.

Online Services

The current App Store build is offline-first and does not require account login. If a future optional online quality mode is added, the app will clearly ask before sending content to an online service.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the developer contact information provided with the App Store listing.
