Neravia AI home · Privacy Policy

Getting Started

Open Neravia AI, choose your app language, then use Chat, Translate, Summarize, Code, or Create from the main workspace. The compact assistant runs on device and does not require account login.

Offline Use

Neravia AI is designed for private on-device work. The compact offline engine can answer prompts, help with writing, summarize notes, and prepare simple translations without sending your prompts to a server.

Languages

The app includes a 20-language menu. If the interface appears in the wrong language, open Settings inside the app and choose the preferred language again.

Purchases

New users receive 2 days of included access. Pro access is a one-time lifetime unlock handled by Apple through the App Store. Purchases can be restored from the app settings.

Troubleshooting

If a button does not respond, close and reopen the app, then try again. If the problem continues, include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the screen where the issue happened when contacting support.
