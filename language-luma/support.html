Language Luma Support

Support for the Language Luma vocabulary apps on iPhone, iPad, and Mac:

  • DeutschLuma UA, PolLuma UA, CeskaLuma UA, HispanoLuma UA, and SuomiLuma UA
  • DutchLuma AR, SvenskLuma AR, DanskLuma AR, SuomiLuma AR, and IvriLuma AR

Using the Apps

  • Tap Start Learning to begin a flashcard session.
  • Mark a word as known or schedule it for review.
  • Use Words to search the full 2,500-card library.
  • Use Favorites and custom cards to create a personal study list.
  • Pronunciation uses voices installed on the device.

Backup and Restore

Open Settings and choose export or import. The system Files picker lets you save a backup to Google Drive when Google Drive is enabled in Files. The apps do not sign in to Google, cannot read other Drive files, and do not use iCloud storage.

Purchase

Each app includes two days of full introductory access. A one-time Apple In-App Purchase then unlocks that app for life. This is not a subscription. The App Store displays the current local price before confirmation. Use Restore Purchase when reinstalling with the same Apple ID.
