Language Luma Support

Support for DeutschLuma UA, PolLuma UA, and DutchLuma AR on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Using the Apps

  • Tap Start Learning to begin a flashcard session.
  • Mark a word as known or schedule it for review.
  • Use Words to search the full 2,500-card library.
  • Use Favorites and custom cards to create a personal study list.
  • Pronunciation uses voices installed on the device.

Backup and Restore

Open Settings and choose export or import. The system Files picker lets you save a backup to On My iPhone, On My Mac, or Google Drive when Google Drive is enabled in Files.

Purchase

Each app includes two days of introductory access. A one-time $4.99 Apple In-App Purchase then unlocks that app for life. This is not a subscription. Use Restore Purchase when reinstalling with the same Apple ID.
