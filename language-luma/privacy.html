Language Luma Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

This policy applies to the Language Luma flashcard apps: DeutschLuma UA, PolLuma UA, CeskaLuma UA, HispanoLuma UA, SuomiLuma UA, DutchLuma AR, SvenskLuma AR, DanskLuma AR, SuomiLuma AR, and IvriLuma AR.

Data Collection

The apps do not collect personal data, do not track users, and do not sell or share user data with third parties.

Local App Data

Study progress, favorites, custom cards, trial status, and preferences are stored locally on the user's device. We do not receive this information.

Backups

A user can export or import a backup with the system Files picker. The user chooses the destination, which may include Google Drive when it is enabled in Files. The apps do not access a cloud account directly and do not use iCloud storage.

Pronunciation

Word pronunciation uses speech features available on the device. The apps do not record the user.

Purchases

The apps offer a one-time lifetime unlock through Apple In-App Purchase after a two-day introductory access period. Apple processes the purchase. We do not receive payment card details.
