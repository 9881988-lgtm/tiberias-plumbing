Tavora AI Translator Support

Offline-first translator for iPhone

Getting Started

Open Tavora, choose the app language, then download the required language packs for offline use. After packs are installed, everyday text and voice translation can work without Wi-Fi, mobile data, or a MacBook connection.

Offline Packs

If a language pack does not download, check free device storage, connect to Wi-Fi for the initial download, and try again from the Packs screen. Once installed, the pack can be used offline.

Voice Mode

Choose the translation direction first, then open Voice. Tavora listens, keeps the transcript on screen, and shows the translated text in a separate scrollable panel.
