Tavora AI Translator Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

Summary

Tavora AI Translator is designed as an offline-first translator. When offline mode is used, translated text, recognized speech, language packs, and history stay on the device and are not sent to our servers.

Data Collection

Tavora does not collect personal data for tracking or advertising. The app privacy manifest declares no collected data types and no tracking.

Offline Translation

Offline text and voice translation run on the iPhone after the required language packs are downloaded. In offline mode, text and audio do not leave the device.

Language Packs

Language packs may be downloaded to the device so translation can work without internet access. Pack availability, size, and quality can vary by language pair.

Local History

If translation history or favorites are enabled, they are stored locally on the device. Users can delete local history from inside the app.

Purchases

Lifetime access purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Tavora does not receive or store payment card information.

Online Services

If a future online quality mode is enabled, the app will ask for clear consent before sending text or audio to an online translation service. Offline mode remains the primary use case.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the developer contact information provided with the App Store listing.
